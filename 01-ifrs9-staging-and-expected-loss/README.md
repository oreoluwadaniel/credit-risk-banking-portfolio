# CreditRisk360: Credit Risk & Portfolio Analytics

**A SQL credit risk portfolio built to answer one practical question: where is credit risk building before it becomes a loss?**

A loan portfolio rarely goes from healthy to default overnight. Delinquency rises, PD changes, loans move into higher-risk stages, collateral coverage weakens, and risk starts concentrating in particular segments.

CreditRisk360 turns those signals into decision-ready analysis across the credit lifecycle.
